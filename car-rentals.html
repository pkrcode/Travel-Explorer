Car Rental Tips

Make the most of your rental experience

Required Documents

  • Valid driver's license
  • International driving permit (if required)
  • Credit card in driver's name
  • Passport or ID

Booking Tips

  • Book early for better rates
  • Compare different car categories
  • Check fuel policy options
  • Review insurance coverage

Before You Drive

  • Inspect the vehicle thoroughly
  • Take photos of any damage
  • Check fuel level and mileage
  • Familiarize with controls

During Your Trip

  • Follow local traffic rules
  • Keep rental agreement handy
  • Park in safe, legal areas
  • Contact us for any issues
